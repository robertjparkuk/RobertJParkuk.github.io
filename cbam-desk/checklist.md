Under both the EU and UK CBAM, embedded emissions are counted inside fixed system boundaries and over a calendar year. A product carbon footprint or an Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) is not CBAM data: it uses different boundaries and is not verified the CBAM way. The source data is often the same, though, so this list is a good place to start.

## If you make CBAM goods (producers and processors)

- **Site boundary and processes.** Which processes at your site make each CBAM good, set against the system boundaries in the rules.
- **Fuels, by calendar year.** Each fuel by type and quantity, with its emission factor and net calorific value.
- **Process emissions.** Any emissions from chemical reactions, not burning fuel: calcining, reduction, nitric acid and so on.
- **Heat and waste gases.** Measurable heat and waste gases flowing in and out of the site.
- **Electricity.** Electricity used. It counts for EU cement and fertilisers only; it does not count for steel, aluminium or hydrogen under the EU rules, and not at all under the UK rules before 2029 at the earliest.
- **Precursors.** For each bought-in material that is itself a CBAM good: commodity code, mass, country of origin, producer, and either its verified emissions or the default value. Under the EU rules, EU-made precursors count as zero.
- **Output.** Saleable output of each CBAM good over the same calendar year.
- **Carbon price paid.** Any carbon price paid, net of free allowances and rebates.
- **Monitoring plan.** A written monitoring plan, in English, for the verifier.

## If you import CBAM goods into the UK

- **Commodity code** (eight or ten digits) for every CBAM good.
- **Value** of each consignment, to run the £50,000 test: on any day, the next 30 days; on the first of each month, the last 12 months.
- **Country of origin** of every consignment. This includes goods from Ireland or the EU delivered into Northern Ireland without a customs declaration: count them from your invoices.
- **Net mass** of each consignment.
- **Supplier emissions data:** verified actual emissions for each installation, or a note that you will use HMRC's default values.
- **Carbon price relief evidence:** HMRC's carbon pricing verification form, completed by a verifier for the producing installation, if you plan to claim relief.
- **Records:** keep all of this for six years from 1 January 2027.

## If your EU customers import from you (GB and non-UK producers)

- Everything in the producer list, set out in the Commission's operator template.
- The name of an accredited verifier: for EU declarations, one accredited by an EU or EEA national accreditation body.
- Whether to register your site in the EU CBAM Registry, so every EU customer can draw on the same data.

Goods made in Northern Ireland are outside the EU CBAM, so an NI producer's EU customers make no CBAM declaration on them.
