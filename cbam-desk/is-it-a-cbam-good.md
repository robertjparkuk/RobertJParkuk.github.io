Match the first four digits of the commodity code on your invoice or customs entry. "EU now" means the EU CBAM applies to imports into the EU today. "UK 2027" means the UK CBAM applies to imports into the UK, including Northern Ireland, from 1 January 2027. Some eight- and ten-digit codes within a heading are excepted, so always confirm the exact code on GOV.UK or EUR-Lex.

## Proposed for 2028 (EU only, not law)

The European Commission has proposed adding about 180 steel- and aluminium-heavy downstream products to the EU CBAM from 1 January 2028. They include machinery parts, fabrications, vehicle components and appliances. The Council and Parliament each want a longer list, and the final list is not settled ([COM(2025) 989](https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/procedure-file?reference=2025%2F0419%28COD%29)). The UK has no equivalent proposal. Goods made in Northern Ireland are outside the EU CBAM either way (European Commission CBAM Q&A, Q 7.14).
