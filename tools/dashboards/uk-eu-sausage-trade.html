Notes on the data

Code 1601 covers all sausages: fresh, cooked, dried and cured, so it includes salami and chorizo as well as the fresh British banger. The EU ban since 2021 applies to chilled fresh sausages and other meat preparations from Great Britain, which are only part of this code. From 2021 Northern Ireland's trade with the EU is recorded differently from Great Britain's. Trade between Great Britain and Northern Ireland is not in these figures at all, so the "sausage war" over GB-to-NI supply does not show here. Values are in cash terms, not adjusted for inflation.

Source: HMRC Overseas Trade Statistics (uktradeinfo), retrieved 16 November 2026; Overseas trade statistics: methodology and quality reports (HMRC, GOV.UK)
