The figures

The budget ladder

Each budget is an average annual cut on the 1990 baseline. Levels in MtCO2e are derived from DAERA's 2024 figure and its 29.4% fall, which gives a base year of about 26.1 Mt. The legal test is the net emissions account averaged over each five-year period, so read this as an indication, not a compliance verdict.

What this means for a business

The Act puts no direct duty on private firms. It reaches them through what government does to close the gap: sector plans for agriculture, transport, buildings and industry, public procurement, grants and regulation. Knowing your own baseline is the first step.
