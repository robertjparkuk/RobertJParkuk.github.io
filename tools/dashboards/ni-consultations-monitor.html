Public consultations, calls for evidence, statutory consultations and engagement surveys run by a council, a Stormont department or a UK Government department whose scope includes Northern Ireland. Planning applications are not counted: they are on the NI Planning Portal.
Public-realm relevance
My judgement of how far each consultation bears on public space, land, streets, water, drainage and infrastructure: high, medium or low. It is a reading aid, not a ranking of importance.
Response published
An outcome report, summary of responses or ministerial statement on the result was found on the body’s pages. "Awaiting" means the consultation closed recently or the body says a report is coming. "None found" means nothing was found when checked, which may mean it is still in preparation.
Verified
The opening and closing dates were read on the consulting body’s own page. Unverified rows rest on a listing, a council paper or a press report, and should be checked before you rely on them.
