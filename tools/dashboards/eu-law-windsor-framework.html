How to read this page

Windsor Framework
The 2023 UK–EU agreement that replaced the Northern Ireland Protocol. Its annexes list the EU laws on goods, customs, VAT on goods, animal and plant health, state aid and the electricity market that apply in NI.
Dynamic alignment (Article 13(3))
When the EU amends or replaces a listed law, the new version applies in NI automatically, unless the Stormont Brake is used.
New EU acts (Article 13(4))
An EU law that is new, not a replacement, applies in NI only if the UK and EU add it by a Joint Committee decision, normally after the Assembly passes an applicability motion.
Stormont Brake
Thirty MLAs from at least two parties can notify the UK Government that an amended or replacement EU law should not apply in NI. It must be a last resort, and the change must be significantly different and affect everyday life. The Secretary of State decides. Used once, on chemical labelling; not applied.
Democratic Scrutiny Committee
The Assembly committee that examines each amending or replacement EU act and can hold an inquiry. Its reports and the Brake deadlines are on the Assembly's EU law scrutiny tracker.
UK Internal Market Scheme and "not at risk" goods
Authorised traders moving goods from GB to NI that will not enter the EU use simplified information instead of a customs declaration. Whether goods are "at risk" decides how much EU customs law bites on a shipment.
NI Retail Movement Scheme
The simplified route for prepacked food and drink moving from GB to NI shops and caterers. Goods carry "Not for EU" labels.

Sources: The Stormont Brake (NI Assembly); UK Internal Market Scheme (Department for the Economy); Retail Movement Scheme (GOV.UK).
