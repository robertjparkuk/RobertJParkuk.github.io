Often assumed, but not applying in NI

These are not in the Windsor Framework annexes, though NI firms can still be caught through EU customers.

EU lawApplies in NI?Why
EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (EU) 2023/956NoNot applied in NI. The UK CBAM covers NI from 1 Jan 2027
Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (EU) 2022/2464NoNot in the Windsor Framework annexes. NI firms are caught only through EU subsidiaries or EU turnover
Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (EU) 2024/1760NoNot in the annexes. Reaches NI firms through EU customers
Nature Restoration Regulation (EU) 2024/1991NoNot in the annexes

Packaging (PPWR)Packaging sold in NI must meet EU design and labelling rules; GB has not decided whether to followNI from 12 Aug 2026
BatteriesEU rules in NI; UK to consult on an aligned UK-wide regimeNI enforcement approved 15 Sep 2026
F-gasGB and NI quotas diverge; GB suppliers to NI must meet EU bansGap widens 1 Jan 2027

About this page

A dated snapshot, checked on 24–25 October 2026 against the NI Assembly's EU law scrutiny tracker and Brexit and Beyond newsletters, GOV.UK, DAERA, the Council of the EU, the European Commission and gov.ie. "Not in the annexes" rows are my reading of the Windsor Framework annexes, not legal advice. Commission close dates are usually midnight Brussels time; check the consultation page before you rely on one. Nothing on this page is fetched or sent, and every table can be downloaded as CSV.
