Planning applications

Planning applications are not counted in this monitor. They are published on the NI Planning Portal, which serves the Department for Infrastructure and 10 of the 11 councils. Only applications currently open for comment appear online; the full statutory register is not online. Mid Ulster runs its own portal.

Data, method and corrections

Each consultation was found on the body's own website, the NI Citizen Space portals or GOV.UK, and checked against the consultation's own page where it would load. "Verified" means its dates were read on that page; unverified rows rest on listings, council papers or press reports. "No response found" means no outcome report could be found, not that none exists. Relevance to the public realm is my judgement. Planning applications are not included.

Corrections and right of reply. Councils and departments are welcome to send corrections, or links to outcome reports I missed, to robert@robertjpark.co.uk. Corrections are made promptly and noted on the findings page.
