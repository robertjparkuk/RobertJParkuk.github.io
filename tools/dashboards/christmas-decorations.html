Christmas decorations: rules, costs and carbon

How the decorations in Northern Ireland's homes, shops and streets are regulated, what they cost to run and what they emit, and where the rules in Northern Ireland, Great Britain and the EU differ. Read the commentary: Deck the halls, check the rules. Not legal, tax or customs advice.
