NI metals and manufacturing

NI industry emissions fell from 3.3 to 1.0 MtCO2e between the base year and 2024. NI businesses sold £20.1bn to Great Britain and £14.1bn to the EU in 2024. From 1 January 2027 the UK carbon border levy applies to imported steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser and hydrogen.
