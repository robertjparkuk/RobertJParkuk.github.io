NI hospitality, retail and leisure

Overnight trips in Northern Ireland rose 9% to 5.1 million in 2025 and spend reached £1.2bn, but hotel occupancy fell to 65%. Heating and refrigeration sit in the buildings sector of the NI inventory, which still emits 2.8 MtCO2e a year.
