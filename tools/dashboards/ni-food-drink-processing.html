NI food and drink processing

Northern Ireland food and drink processors turned over an estimated £7.1bn in 2023, with almost half of sales going to Great Britain. Industry emissions, where factory fuel is counted, are down 69% since 1990. The pressures now are customers, deforestation rules, packaging fees and deposit return.
