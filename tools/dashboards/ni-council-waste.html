What this means for a business

Councils are paid from packaging EPR for household packaging, and a deposit return scheme for drinks containers is due in October 2027. If you make or import packaged goods, your packaging data now drives both your fees and your council's income. If you run a site, the same separation-at-source habits that lift a council's rate cut your own waste bill.
