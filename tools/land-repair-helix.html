Your site
Important. For contaminated land, forever chemicals (PFAS) or any spill reaching a drain, watercourse or groundwater, get specialist advice and involve the regulator. In NI, report pollution to the NIEA Incident Hotline on 0800 80 70 60 (24 hours). Contaminated-land remediation normally goes through planning, using the Land Contamination: Risk Management (LCRM) framework.
