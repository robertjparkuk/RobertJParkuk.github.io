The Carbon Jigsaw

A rough carbon checker for Northern Ireland businesses. Fill in each piece from your bills and records. Pieces you can't fill stay missing, and those gaps tell you as much as the total does.

Your figures stay in this browser. Nothing is sent or stored on a server.
