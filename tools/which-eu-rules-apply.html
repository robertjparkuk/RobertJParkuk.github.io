Which EU rules apply to me?

Answer three questions about what you make and where it goes. You get the EU and UK rules from the EU law and the Windsor Framework dashboard that matter to you, with dates and official sources. As at 13 November 2026. Nothing you tick leaves your browser.

1. What do you make, sell or handle?

Tick all that apply.

2. Where do your goods go?

Tick all that apply.

3. Are the goods you bring from Great Britain "at risk" of moving on into the EU?

Goods are "not at risk" when an authorised trader under the UK Internal Market Scheme declares they will stay in the UK.

About this tool

A guide, not legal advice. It covers the selected laws on the EU law dashboard (environment, carbon, products and goods), not every EU law that applies in Northern Ireland, and it does not cover tax, employment or data rules. It is rebuilt from the dashboard's data each time the dashboard is updated, every Friday. Check the official source on each result before you act.
