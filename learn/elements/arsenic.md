## Climate and carbon

No direct climate role. Gallium arsenide makes efficient solar cells for satellites.

## Land, soil and water

Arsenic is natural in some groundwater and a legacy of sheep dip, timber treatment, mining and old pesticides. It is a common contaminant in site investigations.

## Health and safety

Inorganic arsenic causes skin lesions and cancers; drinking water is limited to 10 µg/l. Rice can carry more than other cereals. Arsenic compounds: Acute Tox. 3, Carc. 1A. Arsine gas is extremely toxic (UN2188). UN1558 (arsenic).

## Industry and supply

Semiconductors and, historically, pesticides and wood preservative (CCA).

## In Northern Ireland

Old sheep-dip sites on farms are a known source of arsenic in soil.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Scheele's green | copper arsenite wallpaper pigment, suspected of poisoning Victorians |
| Arsine | an extremely toxic gas |
| Arsenic trioxide | now a leukaemia treatment |
| Salvarsan | the first modern targeted drug, against syphilis, in 1910 |

## Discovery

Known in antiquity; isolated by Albertus Magnus in about 1250. Stable.
