## Climate and carbon

Capacitors in power electronics.

## Land, soil and water

Coltan mining in central Africa has damaged forests and gorilla habitat.

## Health and safety

Very low toxicity; used in implants. Powder is flammable.

## Industry and supply

Capacitors in phones and laptops, implants, chemical plant.

## Engineering

Tantalum capacitors store a lot of charge in a tiny volume.

## Politics and trade

A regulated conflict mineral under EU and US rules.

## Commerce

Supply chains need due-diligence records.

## Quirks

Named after Tantalus because it cannot absorb acid, as Tantalus could never drink.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Coltan | the ore, columbite-tantalite |
| Tantalum carbide | one of the highest-melting compounds |

## Discovery

Discovered by Anders Ekeberg in 1802; named after Tantalus. Stable.
