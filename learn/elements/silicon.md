## Climate and carbon

Silicon is the base of nearly all solar panels. Making silicon metal from quartz and coal is electricity and carbon intensive.

## Land, soil and water

Quartz sand and silicate minerals make up most soils. Grasses take up silica to stiffen their leaves.

## Health and safety

Respirable crystalline silica from cutting stone, concrete and engineered worktops causes silicosis and lung cancer; the UK workplace limit is 0.1 mg/m³. Respirable crystalline silica WEL 0.1 mg/m³ (EH40). Silicon powder is flammable.

## Industry and supply

Chips, solar cells, glass, concrete, silicones and aluminium alloys. Silicon is on the UK critical minerals list.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Quartz | the commonest mineral in sand |
| Silicones | flexible silicon–oxygen polymers in sealants and bakeware |
| Silica gel | the drying beads in shoe boxes |
| Silicon carbide | harder than almost anything, and used in EV power electronics |

## Discovery

Isolated by Jöns Jacob Berzelius in 1824; from Latin 'silex', flint. Stable.
