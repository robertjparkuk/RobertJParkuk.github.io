## Climate and carbon

Used in some thin-film solar cells (CIGS).

## Land, soil and water

Many UK and Irish soils are low in selenium, so grass and grain are too; livestock are given selenium supplements. Too much, from coal ash or irrigation drainage, has poisoned birds and fish.

## Health and safety

Essential in small amounts; toxic in excess. Brazil nuts are a rich source. Selenium and compounds: Acute Tox. 3, STOT RE 2. UN2658 (selenium powder).

## Industry and supply

Glass decolouriser, electronics, pigments and feed supplements.

## In Northern Ireland

White muscle disease in lambs and calves is linked to low selenium in grass, common on Irish and Northern Irish soils.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Selenomethionine | the form in Brazil nuts and supplements |
| Hydrogen selenide | smells worse than hydrogen sulfide and is far more toxic |
| Selenium sulfide | anti-dandruff shampoo |
| Cadmium selenide | quantum dots in vivid TV screens |

## Discovery

Discovered by Berzelius in 1817; named after Selene, the Moon. Stable.
