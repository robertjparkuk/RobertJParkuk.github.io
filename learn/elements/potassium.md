## Climate and carbon

Potash mining and transport carry energy costs. Potassium-40 gives every body and every banana a small natural radioactivity.

## Land, soil and water

A major crop nutrient, plentiful in slurry. Too much potassium in grass blocks magnesium uptake and raises the risk of grass tetany.

## Health and safety

Essential for nerves and heartbeat; too high or too low a level affects the heart. Potassium metal reacts violently with water. The metal reacts violently with water (H260). UN2257.

## Industry and supply

Potash fertiliser, soaps, glass, food salt substitutes.

## Engineering

Potassium hydroxide is the electrolyte in alkaline batteries.

## Politics and trade

Belarus and Russia are major potash producers, so sanctions affect fertiliser supply.

## Commerce

Potash is mined under North Yorkshire.

## Quirks

Bananas are slightly radioactive because of potassium-40.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Potassium chloride | the main potash fertiliser; injected into a sea urchin it makes it release eggs or sperm, a classic school fertilisation experiment |
| Potassium nitrate | saltpetre, the oxidiser in gunpowder and a meat cure |
| Potassium permanganate | a purple disinfectant and oxidiser |
| Potassium bitartrate | cream of tartar, the crystals in wine barrels |

## Discovery

Isolated by Humphry Davy in 1807, his first metal by electrolysis; symbol K from Latin 'kalium'. Stable (potassium-40 is a natural radioactive isotope).
