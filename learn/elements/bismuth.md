## Climate and carbon

No significant role.

## Land, soil and water

Low toxicity makes it a replacement for lead in shot and plumbing.

## Health and safety

The least toxic heavy metal; bismuth compounds settle upset stomachs. Not usually classified.

## Industry and supply

Lead-free solder, medicines, cosmetics, fire-sprinkler alloys.

## Engineering

Low-melting bismuth alloys plug fire sprinklers and melt at a set temperature.

## Politics and trade

On the UK critical minerals list.

## Commerce

Mostly a by-product of lead and copper refining.

## Quirks

Grows staircase-shaped crystals with rainbow colours. It is very slightly radioactive, with a half-life a billion times the age of the universe.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Bismuth subsalicylate | pink indigestion remedy |
| Bismuth oxychloride | the shimmer in some cosmetics |

## Discovery

Known in the 1400s; recognised as an element by Claude Geoffroy in 1753. Effectively stable (bismuth-209 half-life about 2 × 10¹⁹ years).
