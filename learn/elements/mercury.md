## Climate and carbon

Coal burning and small-scale gold mining are the main sources of mercury in the air. Mercury travels the globe and falls far from where it was released.

## Land, soil and water

In water and wetlands, bacteria turn mercury into methylmercury, which builds up in fish. Old chlor-alkali works and instrument makers can leave contaminated land.

## Health and safety

Methylmercury damages the developing brain, which is why large predatory fish carry advice for pregnancy. Mercury vapour from spills is also harmful. Mercury: Repr. 1B, Acute Tox. 2 (inhalation), STOT RE 1. UN2809.

## Industry and supply

Being phased out under the Minamata Convention: thermometers, lamps, dental amalgam and batteries.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Methylmercury | the form that builds up in tuna and swordfish |
| Cinnabar | red mercury sulfide, the ore and the pigment vermilion |
| Mercury fulminate | a historic primary explosive in percussion caps |
| Thiomersal | a preservative once used in vaccines |

## Discovery

Known in antiquity; symbol Hg from Greek 'hydrargyrum', liquid silver. Stable.
