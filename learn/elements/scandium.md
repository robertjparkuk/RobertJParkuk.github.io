## Climate and carbon

Scandium-stabilised zirconia is used in solid oxide fuel cells.

## Land, soil and water

Widely scattered at low levels; rarely concentrated enough to mine.

## Health and safety

Little toxicity data. Metal powder is flammable.

## Industry and supply

Aluminium-scandium alloys and stadium floodlights.

## Engineering

A pinch of scandium makes aluminium alloys much stronger and weldable, used in bike frames and aircraft.

## Politics and trade

Supply mostly comes as a by-product, which makes it hard to plan for.

## Commerce

A tiny, expensive market held back by scarce supply.

## Quirks

Mendeleev predicted it as 'eka-boron' before it was found.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Scandium oxide | used in high-intensity lamps |

## Discovery

Discovered by Lars Nilson in 1879, filling a gap Mendeleev had predicted; named after Scandinavia. Stable.
