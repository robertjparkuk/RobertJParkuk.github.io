## Climate and carbon

No direct role.

## Land, soil and water

Follows calcium into shells and bones; strontium isotopes in bones and teeth show where an animal or person grew up.

## Health and safety

Stable strontium is low risk; strontium-90 from fallout is a radiation hazard. Metal reacts with water. Strontium nitrate is an oxidiser (UN1507).

## Industry and supply

Red fireworks and flares, ferrite magnets.

## Engineering

Strontium ferrite magnets are the cheap magnets in loudspeakers and fridge doors.

## Politics and trade

Strontium-90 in children's teeth helped build support for the 1963 nuclear test ban treaty.

## Commerce

A small market.

## Quirks

Named after the Scottish village of Strontian, where it was first found.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Strontium carbonate | the red in fireworks |
| Strontium aluminate | glow-in-the-dark paint |

## Discovery

Identified from a mineral found at Strontian, Scotland, in 1790; isolated by Davy in 1808. Stable.
