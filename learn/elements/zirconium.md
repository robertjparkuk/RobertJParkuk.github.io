## Climate and carbon

Zirconium alloy tubes hold the fuel in nuclear reactors.

## Land, soil and water

Zircon crystals are the oldest dated minerals on Earth, over 4 billion years old.

## Health and safety

Low toxicity; metal powder is flammable. Powder is pyrophoric (UN2008, UN1358).

## Industry and supply

Nuclear cladding, ceramics and foundry sand.

## Engineering

Zirconium barely absorbs neutrons, which is why reactors use it.

## Politics and trade

Nuclear-grade zirconium is controlled as a dual-use material.

## Commerce

Most zircon comes from mineral sand mines in Australia and South Africa.

## Quirks

Cubic zirconia is a cheap diamond imitation that is actually harder to scratch than glass.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Zircon | an ancient, durable gem mineral |
| Zirconia | ceramic knives and dental crowns |

## Discovery

Identified by Klaproth in 1789; isolated by Berzelius in 1824. Stable.
