## Climate and carbon

Gallium nitride makes efficient LEDs and power electronics for chargers, inverters and EVs.

## Land, soil and water

Very dispersed; recovered from bauxite and zinc processing.

## Health and safety

Low toxicity. Liquid just above room temperature; corrodes aluminium (UN2803). Metal is Skin Corr./Eye Dam. by many suppliers.

## Industry and supply

LEDs, radio-frequency chips and power electronics.

## Engineering

Gallium nitride chargers are smaller and run cooler than silicon ones.

## Politics and trade

China dominates refining and has controlled exports since 2023; a ban on exports to the US was suspended from 9 November 2025 to 27 November 2026.

## Commerce

A small market with outsized strategic weight; on the UK critical minerals list.

## Quirks

Melts in your hand (about 30 °C). It is used in 'disappearing spoon' tricks because a gallium spoon melts in hot tea.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Gallium nitride | blue LEDs and fast chargers |
| Gallium arsenide | high-efficiency solar cells on satellites |

## Discovery

Discovered by Paul-Émile Lecoq de Boisbaudran in 1875, as Mendeleev predicted; named after Gallia (France). Stable.
