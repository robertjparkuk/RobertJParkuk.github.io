## Climate and carbon

Molybdenum strengthens steel for wind turbines and pipelines; molybdenum catalysts remove sulfur from fuels.

## Land, soil and water

Clover's root bacteria need molybdenum to fix nitrogen. Too much in pasture locks up copper and causes scouring in cattle.

## Health and safety

An essential trace nutrient for people and stock. Molybdenum trioxide: Carc. 2.

## Industry and supply

Stainless and tool steels, catalysts and lubricants.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Molybdenum disulfide | a dry lubricant like graphite |
| Nitrogenase | the enzyme that fixes nitrogen, with a molybdenum heart |
| Ammonium molybdate | used to test water for phosphate |
| Molybdenite | the ore, once confused with lead and graphite |

## Discovery

Identified by Carl Scheele in 1778; isolated by Peter Hjelm in 1781. Stable.
