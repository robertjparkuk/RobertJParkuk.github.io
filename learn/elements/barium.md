## Climate and carbon

Barite weights drilling mud for oil, gas and geothermal wells.

## Land, soil and water

Barite veins occur in many ore fields.

## Health and safety

Soluble barium salts are poisonous; insoluble barium sulfate is safe enough to swallow for X-rays. Soluble barium compounds: Acute Tox. 4. UN1564.

## Industry and supply

Drilling mud, X-ray contrast, glass and green fireworks.

## Engineering

Barium titanate is the material inside most ceramic capacitors.

## Politics and trade

None of note.

## Commerce

Demand follows drilling activity.

## Quirks

Bologna stone, a barium mineral, was the first known glow-in-the-dark material, in 1603.

## Discovery

Isolated by Humphry Davy in 1808; from Greek 'barys', heavy. Stable.
