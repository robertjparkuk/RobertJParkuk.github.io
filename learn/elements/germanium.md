## Climate and carbon

Used in high-efficiency solar cells for satellites.

## Land, soil and water

Traces in zinc ores and some coals.

## Health and safety

Low toxicity; some 'health' supplements have caused kidney damage. Powder is flammable.

## Industry and supply

Fibre-optic glass and infrared lenses.

## Engineering

Germanium lenses pass infrared light, so thermal cameras use them.

## Politics and trade

Under the same Chinese export controls as gallium.

## Commerce

Supply concentrated in China.

## Quirks

The first transistor, in 1947, was made of germanium.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Germanium dioxide | added to optical fibre glass |

## Discovery

Discovered by Clemens Winkler in 1886; named after Germany. Stable.
