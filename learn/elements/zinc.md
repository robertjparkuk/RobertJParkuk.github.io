## Climate and carbon

Galvanising with zinc keeps steel from rusting for decades, so less steel needs replacing. Zinc batteries are researched as a safer storage option.

## Land, soil and water

Zinc washes off galvanised roofs, crash barriers and tyres into drains and is toxic to aquatic life. Pig slurry and sewage sludge can raise soil zinc.

## Health and safety

An essential nutrient for immunity and growth. Breathing zinc fume from welding galvanised steel causes 'metal fume fever'. Zinc powder (stabilised): Aquatic Acute/Chronic 1 (harmonised); powder reacts with water (UN1436).

## Industry and supply

Galvanising, die-cast parts, brass, rubber and sunscreen.

## Engineering

Galvanised steel can last decades outdoors without paint.

## Politics and trade

Ireland's zinc mining makes it one of Europe's main zinc producers.

## Commerce

Zinc prices follow construction and car-making.

## In Northern Ireland

Ireland's Tara mine, near Navan, is one of Europe's largest zinc mines.

## Quirks

Zinc is in brass, and brass instruments are mostly copper and zinc.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Zinc oxide | white sunscreen and nappy cream |
| Zinc sulfide | glows in the dark when mixed with copper |
| Brass | copper and zinc |
| Zinc phosphide | a rodenticide that releases phosphine in the stomach |

## Discovery

Used in brass since antiquity; isolated in Europe by Andreas Marggraf in 1746. Stable.
