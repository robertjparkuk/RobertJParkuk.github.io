## Climate and carbon

Chlorine from CFCs destroyed stratospheric ozone; CFCs are also strong greenhouse gases. The Montreal Protocol phased them out, the most successful environmental treaty so far.

## Land, soil and water

Road salt and effluent raise chloride in rivers. Persistent organochlorines (DDT, PCBs, dioxins) build up in soils, sediments and food chains.

## Health and safety

Chlorinating drinking water ended cholera and typhoid outbreaks. Chlorine gas damages lungs; never mix bleach with acid cleaners. Chlorine: Press. Gas, Acute Tox. 3 H331, Aquatic Acute 1. Never mix bleach with acid. UN1017.

## Industry and supply

Disinfection, PVC, solvents, medicines and bleach all start from salt split by electricity.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Sodium hypochlorite | household bleach |
| PVC | pipes, window frames and cable sheathing |
| Chloroform | one of the first surgical anaesthetics |
| Sucralose | a chlorinated sugar 600 times sweeter than sugar |

## Discovery

Prepared by Carl Scheele in 1774; shown to be an element by Humphry Davy in 1810; from Greek 'chloros', pale green. Stable.
