## Climate and carbon

Cuts nitrogen oxides in petrol exhausts.

## Land, soil and water

Converter metals collect on roadsides.

## Health and safety

Low toxicity as metal. Some compounds are sensitisers.

## Industry and supply

Three-way catalysts and glass-fibre manufacture.

## Engineering

Rhodium plating gives white gold its bright finish.

## Politics and trade

Supply comes mostly from South Africa.

## Commerce

One of the rarest and most volatile precious-metal markets.

## Quirks

Thieves target catalytic converters for the rhodium, palladium and platinum inside.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Rhodium catalysts | used to make acetic acid and medicines |

## Discovery

Discovered by William Hyde Wollaston in 1803; from Greek 'rhodon', rose. Stable.
