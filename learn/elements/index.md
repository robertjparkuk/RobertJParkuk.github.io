| 1 | H | [Hydrogen]({{ '/learn/elements/hydrogen/' | relative_url }}) | Low-carbon fuel and feedstock for fertiliser and steel. Not a greenhouse gas itself, but leaks extend methane's life in the air. |

| 7 | N | [Nitrogen]({{ '/learn/elements/nitrogen/' | relative_url }}) | Grass's main nutrient and Northern Ireland's main air and water pollutant from farming: ammonia, nitrate and the greenhouse gas nitrous oxide. |

| 11 | Na | [Sodium]({{ '/learn/elements/sodium/' | relative_url }}) | Salt. Road salt raises chloride in streams and damages verges; too much in the diet raises blood pressure. Sodium-ion batteries are emerging. |

| 13 | Al | [Aluminium]({{ '/learn/elements/aluminium/' | relative_url }}) | Energy-hungry metal covered by CBAM. In acid soils and water it turns toxic to roots and fish; liming locks it up. |

| 15 | P | [Phosphorus]({{ '/learn/elements/phosphorus/' | relative_url }}) | Essential crop nutrient and the main cause of Lough Neagh's algal blooms. Northern Ireland's farms run a phosphorus surplus. |
| 16 | S | [Sulfur]({{ '/learn/elements/sulfur/' | relative_url }}) | Once acid rain, now a fertiliser crops lack. Hydrogen sulfide from slurry kills; SF₆ in switchgear is the strongest greenhouse gas in common use. |

| 19 | K | [Potassium]({{ '/learn/elements/potassium/' | relative_url }}) | Major crop nutrient (potash) and plentiful in slurry. Too much in grass blocks magnesium uptake in livestock. |
| 20 | Ca | [Calcium]({{ '/learn/elements/calcium/' | relative_url }}) | Limestone, chalk and lime. Liming fixes acid soils; cement and lime making release CO₂ and are covered by CBAM. |
| 21 | Sc | Scandium *(coming soon)* | Rare metal in light aluminium alloys and fuel cells. No soil, climate or health role of note. |
| 22 | Ti | Titanium *(coming soon)* | Light, strong metal; its oxide whitens paint. Titanium dioxide was banned as a food additive in the EU in 2022. |
| 23 | V | Vanadium *(coming soon)* | Strengthens steel and stores renewable power in flow batteries. Released by burning heavy oil; its pentoxide is a suspected carcinogen. |

| 29 | Cu | [Copper]({{ '/learn/elements/copper/' | relative_url }}) | Wiring for electrification. Toxic to aquatic life; builds up in soils from pig slurry and fungicides; too much poisons sheep. |
