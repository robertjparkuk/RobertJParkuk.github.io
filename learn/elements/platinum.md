## Climate and carbon

Diesel catalysts and hydrogen fuel cells.

## Land, soil and water

Builds up in roadside soils.

## Health and safety

Platinum salts cause asthma in workers; cisplatin is a major cancer drug. Soluble platinum salts are respiratory sensitisers; WEL 0.002 mg/m³ (EH40).

## Industry and supply

Catalysts, fuel cells, electrodes, jewellery.

## Engineering

Platinum thermometers are the standard for precise temperature measurement.

## Politics and trade

South Africa and Russia supply most of the world's platinum.

## Commerce

Demand may shift from diesel cars to fuel cells and electrolysers.

## Quirks

Spanish explorers once threw platinum away as worthless 'little silver'. The kilogram was defined by a platinum-iridium cylinder until 2019.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Cisplatin | a cancer drug discovered by accident in 1965 |
| Platinum black | a fine powder catalyst |

## Discovery

Used in pre-Columbian South America; described in Europe by Antonio de Ulloa in 1748; from Spanish 'platina', little silver. Stable.
