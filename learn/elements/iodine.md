## Climate and carbon

Iodine released from the ocean affects ozone in the lower atmosphere.

## Land, soil and water

Coastal soils are richer in iodine from sea spray; inland upland soils can be low.

## Health and safety

Essential for thyroid hormones; deficiency causes goitre and lowers IQ. Potassium iodide tablets protect the thyroid after nuclear releases. Iodine: Acute Tox. 4, STOT RE 1 (thyroid), Aquatic Acute 1.

## Industry and supply

Disinfectants, X-ray contrast, iodised salt, LCD screens.

## Engineering

Iodine is used in screen polarisers and in some solar cell research.

## Politics and trade

The UK is one of few European countries without routine iodised salt, and mild deficiency in pregnancy has been reported.

## Commerce

Chile and Japan produce most of it.

## Quirks

First found in seaweed ash, used for making gunpowder in Napoleonic France. Solid iodine turns straight to violet vapour.

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Thyroxine | the thyroid hormone, built with four iodine atoms |
| Povidone-iodine | the brown antiseptic used before surgery |

## Discovery

Discovered by Bernard Courtois in 1811 in seaweed ash; from Greek 'iodes', violet. Stable.
