## Climate and carbon

Solder joins every circuit, including solar inverters and heat-pump controls.

## Land, soil and water

Banned TBT antifouling paint caused sex changes in sea snails; old boatyard sediments can still hold it.

## Health and safety

Inorganic tin is low toxicity; organotins are toxic. Organotins: acutely toxic, Aquatic Chronic 1.

## Industry and supply

Solder, tin plate for food cans, bronze, float glass.

## Engineering

Float glass is made by floating molten glass on a bath of molten tin.

## Politics and trade

Tin from parts of central Africa is a regulated conflict mineral.

## Commerce

Electronics drive demand.

## Quirks

Tin 'cries' with a creaking sound when bent. In cold, it can crumble to powder ('tin pest').

## Remarkable compounds

| Compound | Why it is remarkable |
|---|---|
| Tributyltin | the banned antifouling compound |
| Stannous fluoride | in some toothpastes |

## Discovery

Used since the Bronze Age; symbol Sn from Latin 'stannum'. Stable.
