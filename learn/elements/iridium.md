## Climate and carbon

The catalyst in PEM electrolysers for green hydrogen; supply is very small.

## Land, soil and water

An iridium-rich clay layer marks the asteroid impact that ended the dinosaurs.

## Health and safety

Low toxicity; iridium-192 sources are a radiation hazard and are sometimes lost or stolen. Powder is flammable.

## Industry and supply

Electrolysers, spark plugs and crucibles.

## Engineering

Iridium-192 is used to X-ray welds in pipelines.

## Politics and trade

Supply could limit green hydrogen growth unless less iridium is used.

## Commerce

A tiny market produced as a by-product of platinum.

## Quirks

The most corrosion-resistant metal. The 1980 iridium discovery changed how we think about mass extinctions.

## Discovery

Discovered by Smithson Tennant in 1803; from Greek 'iris', rainbow. Stable.
