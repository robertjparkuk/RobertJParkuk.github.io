## Where the rules stand (24 November 2026)

- **UK CBAM** starts on 1 January 2027 for aluminium, cement, fertiliser, hydrogen, and iron and steel. The registration threshold is £50,000 of CBAM goods. The first return and payment, for 2027, are due by 31 May 2028 ([HMRC policy summary, updated 23 November 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam-policy-summary/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam-policy-summary)).
- **Goods of EU, EEA or Swiss origin.** The UK intends to exempt eligible goods from 1 January 2027. HMRC published [draft legislation](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/draft-legislation-exemption-for-eu-eea-and-swiss-origin-goods-from-cbam) on 23 November 2026 and expects it to become law after that date, with retrospective effect. It is not law yet. Keep your CBAM records and your evidence of origin.
- **EU CBAM** has applied to imports into the EU since 1 January 2026. The EU exemption for UK goods is expected from 1 January 2028 and needs its own EU legal act.
- **Two regimes, still moving.** These tools are updated as the legal texts that follow the UK–EU summit of 20 November 2026 are published. Each page carries the date it was last checked.

These tools read codes, weights, values and bills. They do not decide the origin of goods, who the importer is, or whether you are liable. Whether a load from the Republic of Ireland into Northern Ireland is a UK CBAM import is a customs question: take it to a customs specialist.

## Add it to your phone or tablet

- **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap **Install on this device** above, or open the menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **Mac (Safari):** choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.
- **Laptop or desktop (Chrome or Edge):** use the install icon in the address bar.

Once added, the tools above open without a connection. When you are online you always get the current page.
