## Business clients only

I work for businesses, councils, public bodies and trade associations, not for private individuals acting outside a business. My services are not offered to consumers.

## How a job is agreed

- Every job starts with a written quotation, which holds for 90 days.
- Work begins only when you have accepted a one-page letter of engagement. It sets out the scope, the price, the delivery date, what you supply, payment terms and the limit of my liability.
- If the letter of engagement and this page differ, the letter of engagement applies.
- Where a registered or accredited specialist must sign off work (for example an ESOS Lead Assessor, a UKAS-accredited verifier or a site-investigation laboratory), that is stated in the letter, and you may be asked to appoint them directly.

## What my work is, and is not

My advice is on environmental, carbon, energy and policy questions. It is not legal advice, tax advice, or advice on customs classification or origin. Where you need a solicitor, tax adviser or customs specialist, I say so.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with my work or how I have dealt with you:

1. Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) with "Complaint" in the subject line, saying what went wrong and what you would like me to do.
2. I will acknowledge it within two working days and send a full reply within ten working days.
3. If you are a member of the public with a concern about data, see my [privacy page]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}); you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office.

I am a member of the Chartered Institute of Public Relations and follow its Code of Conduct.
