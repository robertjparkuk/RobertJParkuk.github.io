Public sector and membership organisations face the same carbon and environmental questions as business, with added layers of accountability, procurement rules and public scrutiny. My background spans both worlds: technical environmental work, and more than twenty years of communications, public affairs and governance, much of it in and around the public sector.

## For district councils

<figure class="page-photo"><img src="{{ '/assets/photos/bins-collection.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A refuse collection crew emptying wheelie bins on a street" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Waste, land and public space: the everyday business of local government.</figcaption></figure>

Northern Ireland's eleven councils sit at the sharp end of environmental policy. I can help with:

- **Carbon inventories and baselines** for council operations, estates and fleets, built on recognised methods and easy to update year on year.
- **Climate and sustainability planning**, turning targets into practical, costed actions that members and officers can stand behind.
- **Contaminated land readiness.** If Part III of the Waste and Contaminated Land (NI) Order 1997 is ever commenced, councils would become responsible for inspecting their areas and dealing with problem sites. I can help councils understand what that would involve, with specialist site investigation brought in where needed.
- **Member and officer briefings** on CBAM, CSRD, emissions trading and Windsor Framework issues affecting local businesses.
- **Support for local business programmes**, including workshops and guidance for SMEs facing customer and regulatory demands on carbon.

## For trade bodies and membership organisations

Trade associations are often the first place members turn when new rules appear. I can support you with:

- **Member briefings and guidance notes** on CBAM, CSRD value-chain requests, ESOS and carbon reporting, written for busy owners rather than specialists.
- **Events and webinars**, from short explainers to practical workshops.
- **Consultation responses** that gather member evidence and put it to government clearly and credibly.
- **Policy positioning and stakeholder engagement** with officials, MLAs, councillors and Westminster.

- **Technical depth.** A chemistry and materials science background, sustainability consulting since 2018, and qualifications from the Cambridge Institute for Sustainability Leadership and the Institute of Internal Auditors.
- **Policy and communications.** Member of the Chartered Institute of Public Relations, experienced in consultations, briefings and working with elected representatives and officials.
- **Local and independent.** Based in Larne, working across Northern Ireland, with no products to sell and no conflicts with suppliers.
- **Collaborative delivery.** For specialist elements such as ESOS sign-off or site investigation, I work with registered and accredited specialists and manage the work as a single point of contact. See [who I work with]({{ '/working-with/' | relative_url }}).
