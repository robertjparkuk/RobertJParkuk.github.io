<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/tools-mark.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">CBAM Exposure Tracker</h2>

Who in Northern Ireland pays a carbon border charge, from when, and at what price. Where an NI business stands under the EU CBAM and the UK CBAM (which starts on 1 January 2027), the EU CBAM certificate price for each quarter of 2026, NI goods trade with the EU, and the two regimes' rules side by side. Figures as at 22 October 2026, from the European Commission, HMRC, GOV.UK and the UK Parliament.

[Open the dashboard →]({{ '/tools/dashboards/cbam-exposure-tracker.html' | relative_url }}) · Background: [CBAM and CSRD: what NI manufacturers need to know]({{ '/blog/2026/10/cbam-and-csrd-for-ni-manufacturers/' | relative_url }})

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/tools-mark.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">NI Agriculture and Land Carbon</h2>

Farm and land emissions in Northern Ireland from 1990 to 2024: emissions by source and gas, livestock methane and its share of all NI methane, nitrogen and phosphorus balances, livestock numbers, land use and peatland, F-gases, a Scope 1, 2 and 3 split for the sector, measures to cut emissions, and the research under way. Figures as at 18 October 2026, from the NI greenhouse gas inventory (data updated August 2026), DAERA statistics and AFBI.

[Open the dashboard →]({{ '/tools/dashboards/ni-agriculture-land-carbon.html' | relative_url }}) · Commentary: [Focus on Agriculture, October 2026]({{ '/blog/focus-on-agriculture-october-2026/' | relative_url }})

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/tools-mark.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">NI Food and Drink Processing</h2>

Turnover from 2020, where sales go, NI emissions by sector, what the EU deforestation rules cover and who enforces them, and every deadline for processors to the end of 2027. Figures checked on 21 October 2026, from NISRA, DAERA, GOV.UK and PackUK.
