<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/tools-mark.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">NI Agriculture and Land Carbon</h2>

Farm and land emissions in Northern Ireland from 1990 to 2024: emissions by source and gas, livestock methane and its share of all NI methane, nitrogen and phosphorus balances, livestock numbers, land use and peatland, F-gases, a Scope 1, 2 and 3 split for the sector, measures to cut emissions, and the research under way. Figures as at 18 October 2026, from the NI greenhouse gas inventory (data updated August 2026), DAERA statistics and AFBI.
