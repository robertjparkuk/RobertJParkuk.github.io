<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/tools-mark.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">NI Agriculture and Land Carbon</h2>

Farm and land emissions in Northern Ireland from 1990 to 2024: emissions by source and gas, livestock methane and its share of all NI methane, nitrogen and phosphorus balances, livestock numbers, land use and peatland, F-gases, a Scope 1, 2 and 3 split for the sector, measures to cut emissions, and the research under way. Figures as at 18 October 2026, from the NI greenhouse gas inventory (data updated August 2026), DAERA statistics and AFBI.

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/tools-mark.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">NI Consultation Watch</h2>

Every public consultation open, or closed since 22 April 2025, at Northern Ireland's 11 councils, nine Executive departments and UK Government where NI is in scope: 385 in all. Closing dates, which have a published outcome, a council transparency score, and links to the NI Planning Portal. Snapshot taken 22 October 2026.

[Open the dashboard →]({{ '/tools/dashboards/ni-consultation-watch.html' | relative_url }}) · Findings: [NI Consultation Watch]({{ '/public-sector/consultation-watch/' | relative_url }})
