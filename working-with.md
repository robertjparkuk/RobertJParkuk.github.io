## How each service is delivered

| Service | Who does the work |
| --- | --- |
| Policy, public affairs and consultation responses | Me, start to finish |
| Carbon inventories | Led by me, with carbon accountants for the calculations. The statement of how the inventory and reduction plan were built is checked independently by a UKAS-accredited verifier. |
| CBAM and CSRD | Led by me, with carbon accountants where a job needs embedded-emissions calculations at scale |
| ESOS and energy costs | Led by me, with ESOS Lead Assessors. A registered Lead Assessor signs off every ESOS assessment. |
| Environmental health | Led by me, with a multidisciplinary team as the job needs |
| Forever chemicals (PFAS) | Led by me, with technical support on site operations |
| Contaminated land | Led by me. Site investigation, laboratory testing and remediation are carried out by specialist firms. |
| Water, waste and packaging | Delivered in collaboration with partners, depending on the project |

## The specialists I bring in

- **ESOS Lead Assessors** on an approved professional register (the Energy Institute or IEMA). I work with several, depending on their workload.
- **A UKAS-accredited greenhouse gas verifier** for independent checks of carbon inventories.
- **Geo-environmental and site investigation firms** working in Northern Ireland, with accredited laboratories for soil, water and PFAS testing.
- **Carbon accountants and inventory auditors** for calculation-heavy work.

I name a partner on a proposal before any work starts, so you know exactly who will be on your site and who signs what. Every partner holds their own professional indemnity insurance, and I hold mine for the work I manage.

## Associates

I'm building a small bench of project-based associates from the Imperial College London community: a carbon accountant and inventory auditor, a land remediation analyst, and a parliamentary and policy officer. They work remotely on specific projects under a non-disclosure agreement and a service-level agreement.
