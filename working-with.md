## The specialists I bring in

- **ESOS Lead Assessors** on an approved professional register (the Energy Institute or IEMA). I work with several, depending on their workload.
- **A UKAS-accredited greenhouse gas verifier** for independent checks of voluntary carbon inventories. This accreditation alone does not cover CBAM.
- **CBAM verifiers, which need two separate accreditations.** For EU customers' CBAM declarations, a verifier accredited by an EU or EEA national accreditation body ([Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2551](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32025R2551)). For UK CBAM returns, a verifier accredited for UK CBAM to ISO/IEC 17029 and ISO 14065 by a body that is a full member of Global Accreditation Cooperation ([SI 2026/995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/995/made); [HMRC guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/responsibilities-if-you-accredit-verifiers-for-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam-goods)). Neither accreditation automatically covers the other regime. Where possible I use one firm that holds both, so one site visit serves both. Until data is verified, your customer or you must use default values.
- **Customs advisers and brokers** for commodity-code classification, origin, who the importer is, and EU indirect customs representation.
- **Geo-environmental and site investigation firms** working in Northern Ireland, with accredited laboratories for soil, water and PFAS testing.
- **Carbon accountants and inventory auditors** for calculation-heavy work.

I name a partner on a proposal before any work starts, so you know exactly who will be on your site and who signs what. Every partner holds their own professional indemnity insurance. My own cover for the work I manage is being arranged, and a certificate is supplied before any paid work starts.

## Where I bring in a customs adviser

On CBAM, some points are settled and some are not. I keep them apart.

**Safe to state, with sources:**

- Goods produced or released for free circulation in Northern Ireland are outside the EU CBAM. The European Commission's CBAM Q&A (updated 15 October 2026, Q7.14) says: "Goods produced and/or released for free circulation in Northern Ireland are not subject to CBAM." ([Commission Q&A](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/013fa763-5dce-4726-a204-69fec04d5ce2_en?filename=CBAM_Questions+and+Answers.pdf))
- The UK CBAM applies across the UK, including Northern Ireland, from 1 January 2027. It covers EU-origin goods entering Northern Ireland: the Finance Act 2026 treats "a Union good that is imported into the United Kingdom as a result of its entry into Northern Ireland" as imported, and no Treasury regulations exclude them ([Finance Act 2026, Part 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/11/part/5)).
- Goods of UK origin are not charged, and goods returned within three years can be relieved (Finance Act 2026, s.147).
- Carbon price relief needs a form completed by a verifier for the installation that made the goods ([SI 2026/809](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/809/made)).

**Needs a customs or tax specialist before you act:**

- Who the importer is where there is no customs declaration (Finance Act 2026, s.146(4): "the person on whose behalf the good is imported"), and what delivery terms such as DDP change.
- Goods moving from Northern Ireland to Great Britain that are not qualifying Northern Ireland goods.
- Whether lightly processed EU or GB inputs count as made in Northern Ireland (origin).
- "At risk" or UK Internal Market Scheme routing.
- Any arrangement close to the £50,000 UK threshold. I will not structure imports to stay under it.

HMRC has not yet published guidance on goods arriving in Northern Ireland from Ireland or the EU without a customs declaration. Until it does, I flag that point in every piece of CBAM work.

## Where I send people first

Two free, trusted places for Northern Ireland businesses starting on CBAM:

- **Invest NI** publishes a free CBAM guide and toolkit on [nibusinessinfo.co.uk](https://www.nibusinessinfo.co.uk/content/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam-northern-ireland-businesses). Use it first; I can then check your answers product by product.
- **InterTradeIreland's Cross-Border Trade Hub** covers [the UK CBAM and cross-border trade](https://crossbordertradehub.intertradeireland.com/uk-cbam-what-to-expect-key-dates-and-possible-linkage-to-the-eu) and offers one-to-one support.

HMRC also takes [questions about CBAM](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/send-hmrc-questions-about-the-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam) by email and online.
