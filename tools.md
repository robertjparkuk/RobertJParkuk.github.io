<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/icons/carbon-jigsaw.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">The Carbon Jigsaw</h2>

A rough carbon checker for Northern Ireland businesses. Fill in each piece of the jigsaw from your bills and records: electricity, gas, oil, vehicles, refrigerants, water, waste, travel and commuting. You'll get a rough Scope 1, 2 and partial Scope 3 footprint, and the pieces you can't fill show what a full inventory would add. Your figures stay in your browser. Practise first with the sample bills.

[Open the Carbon Jigsaw →]({{ '/tools/carbon-jigsaw.html' | relative_url }})

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/icons/scopes-map.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">The Scopes Map</h2>

Scope 1, 2 and 3 on one diagram, with all fifteen Scope 3 categories and their technical definitions. It also covers the grey zones clients ask about: gas that leaks before or after the meter, LPG that isn't a counted gas, water from a borehole, grey-fleet mileage, landlord-supplied energy, and which gases count at all.

[Open the Scopes Map →]({{ '/tools/scopes-map.html' | relative_url }})

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/tools-mark.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">Farm Carbon Data Checker</h2>

For Northern Ireland farms asked for carbon data by a processor, supermarket, lender or scheme. Pick your enterprises, then mark each record as something you have, can get or are missing. It shows how ready you are for a Scope 1, 2 and 3 report and exactly where to find each missing record, from APHIS and milk statements to your Soil Nutrient Health Scheme results. It checks records only and doesn't calculate emissions. Your answers stay in your browser, and you can copy or download the checklist.

[Open the Farm Carbon Data Checker →]({{ '/tools/farm-data-checker/' | relative_url }}) · Background: [Focus on Agriculture, October 2026]({{ '/blog/focus-on-agriculture-october-2026/' | relative_url }})

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/tools-mark.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">Dashboards</h2>

Northern Ireland data on one page, from official statistics, with every table downloadable as CSV. **CBAM Exposure Tracker**: where an NI business stands under the EU and UK carbon border charges, the EU certificate price and the rules side by side. **NI Agriculture and Land Carbon**: farm and land emissions from 1990 to 2024, nutrients, livestock, peatland, F-gases and the measures that cut them. **EU Law and the Windsor Framework**: which EU laws apply in NI, Stormont Brake cases, effects on GB and open EU and Irish consultations.

[Open the CBAM Exposure Tracker →]({{ '/tools/dashboards/cbam-exposure-tracker.html' | relative_url }}) · [EU Law and the Windsor Framework →]({{ '/tools/dashboards/eu-law-windsor-framework.html' | relative_url }}) · [All dashboards →]({{ '/dashboards/' | relative_url }})

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/icons/land-repair-helix.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">The Land Repair Helix</h2>

A design tool for healthier land, now with 22 base pairs. Each base pair links a problem (compacted soil, fertiliser overuse, flooding, blue-green algae, contamination, forever chemicals, oil spills, mown-out verges, flailed hedges) to the low-cost or nature-based fix that answers it. Choose your type of site and it builds a starter plan.

[Open the Land Repair Helix →]({{ '/tools/land-repair-helix.html' | relative_url }}) · Printable site plans: [farm]({{ '/tools/site-plans/site-plan-farm.pdf' | relative_url }}), [verge]({{ '/tools/site-plans/site-plan-verge.pdf' | relative_url }}), [brownfield]({{ '/tools/site-plans/site-plan-brown.pdf' | relative_url }}), [factory]({{ '/tools/site-plans/site-plan-factory.pdf' | relative_url }}), [park]({{ '/tools/site-plans/site-plan-park.pdf' | relative_url }}), [river]({{ '/tools/site-plans/site-plan-river.pdf' | relative_url }}) (PDF)

The UK carbon border charge starts on 1 January 2027. A live countdown, the dates that follow for UK and EU CBAM, and five things Northern Ireland importers of steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser and hydrogen should do now.

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/icons/incoterms.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">Incoterms Chooser</h2>

Four questions to find the Incoterms® 2020 rule that matches how you split transport, risk, insurance and customs with your buyer or seller. Includes a who-does-what matrix for all 11 rules, plus notes on GB–NI movements, EU trade and CBAM.

[Open the Incoterms Chooser →]({{ '/tools/incoterms.html' | relative_url }})

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/icons/joined-up-wheel.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">The Joined-Up Wheel</h2>

Eight environmental issues that most firms handle separately: carbon, energy costs, land, waste and packaging, forever chemicals (PFAS), water, environmental health, and policy and public affairs. All of them feed the same business goals. Pick an issue to see what triggers it, the rules today and the routes in. Pick a goal to see which issues it depends on.

[Open the Joined-Up Wheel →]({{ '/tools/joined-up-wheel.html' | relative_url }})

Carbon and sustainability rules across the UK, Northern Ireland, the EU, North America, Australasia and the UN, comparing the position in 2023 with the position now. Filter by market, by status, or by what matters to NI manufacturers and to councils and public bodies.

[Open the ESG Regime Tracker →]({{ '/tools/esg-regime-tracker.html' | relative_url }})

<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/icons/carbon-crossword.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">The Carbon Crossword</h2>

Twenty-four terms from greenhouse gas reporting, carbon pricing and Northern Ireland's climate rules. Easy clues define the term; hard clues assume you've read the guidance. Use it as a team quiz, a workshop warm-up, or a printable handout with the answers on a separate page.

[Play the Carbon Crossword →]({{ '/puzzles/carbon-crossword.html' | relative_url }})


<h2 class="tool-h"><img src="{{ '/assets/icons/planet-check.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="48" height="48">Planet Check: GCSE quiz</h2>

Twenty-eight questions on the atmosphere, water, resources and ecosystems, linked to GCSE science and geography topics, plus a Real world NI set on Lough Neagh, peatlands and NI's climate targets. Nothing is collected or stored, so it's safe for classroom use.

[Take the Planet Check quiz →]({{ '/puzzles/planet-check.html' | relative_url }}) · [Printable worksheet with answers (PDF)]({{ '/puzzles/planet-check-worksheet.pdf' | relative_url }})

Twenty-two everyday business activities to sort into Scope 1, Scope 2, Scope 3 or "not counted". Some are easy; some trip up professionals, like biomass CO₂, butane leaks and a unit billed through the landlord. Every answer comes with the reason.
