## CBAM and CSRD

<figure class="page-photo"><img src="{{ '/assets/photos/shipping-containers.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Stacked shipping containers at a port" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Two carbon regimes, one supply chain.</figcaption></figure>

Northern Ireland manufacturers often trade into both the UK and EU markets, which means navigating two sets of carbon rules at once. I help you work out:

- whether the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) and the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) affect you, directly or through your customers
- what emissions and supply-chain data you'll need, and where to get it
- how to prepare without building a reporting burden you don't need

## Carbon inventories

<figure class="page-photo"><img src="{{ '/assets/photos/food-manufacturing.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Food production workers at a stainless-steel line" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Every product has a footprint behind it.</figcaption></figure>

A carbon inventory measures your organisation's greenhouse gas emissions across its operations and supply chain. Done well, it gives you:

- a credible baseline for customers, lenders and tender requirements
- better conversations with suppliers and customers about where emissions really sit
- non-financial management information your board can use

## ESOS

<figure class="page-photo"><img src="{{ '/assets/photos/plant-room.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Pipework and plant in an industrial plant room" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Most energy savings are found in the plant room, not the report.</figcaption></figure>

I offer the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme as a service, delivered with experienced ESOS Lead Assessors. I recommend running ESOS alongside a carbon inventory and a review of your energy contracts, so the work produces savings rather than just compliance.
