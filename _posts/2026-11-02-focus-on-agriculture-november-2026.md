*Saving agriculture, saving the land: how innovation is building food security and better husbandry in Northern Ireland.*

HMRC's first illustrative UK CBAM rate for fertiliser, £1.45 per tonne of CO2e, is far lower than the trade feared, but the charge still starts on 1 January 2027 and real rates come before Christmas. On the land, a new Nature Recovery Strategy (27 October) and a better-paid Forest Expansion Scheme raise the stakes for how every farm uses its ground.

The long-run figures sit on the companion dashboard, [NI Agriculture and Land Carbon]({{ '/tools/dashboards/ni-agriculture-land-carbon.html' | relative_url }}). Last month's edition: [Focus on Agriculture, October 2026]({{ '/blog/focus-on-agriculture-october-2026/' | relative_url }}).

## Saving the land

The big land news is the [Nature Recovery Strategy to 2032](https://www.daera-ni.gov.uk/news/muir-launches-strategy-restore-nature), launched on 27 October. It backs "30 by 30": 30% of land and inland water managed for nature by 2030. Only about 9.9% of NI land is designated today, so farmland will have to carry much of the gap.

- **Peatland.** No new figures this month. The [Peatland Strategy Delivery Plan](https://www.daera-ni.gov.uk/news/daera-publishes-first-peatland-strategy-delivery-plan-northern-ireland) still aims to put 4,500 ha on the road to recovery by December 2027, towards 150,000 ha by 2050. Land use remains a net source of 2.1 MtCO2e, mostly from degraded peat.
- **Woodland.** The [Forest Expansion Scheme](https://www.daera-ni.gov.uk/news/minister-muir-announces-opening-improved-forest-expansion-scheme) reopened on 7 September with a single premium of £469/ha for 10 years, up from £75–£425 since 2015, and up to 100% of establishment costs. The round closed on 19 October. The need is plain: only 310 ha were planted in 2025/26 against a 600 ha target ([Farmers Journal](https://www.farmersjournal.ie/more/northern-ireland/daera-tree-planting-way-off-target-924769), 22 July). The [Tree Planting Action Plan](https://www.daera-ni.gov.uk/news/muir-publishes-northern-irelands-first-tree-planting-action-plan) aims to lift forest cover from 8.6% to 12% over 25 years.
- **Soils and swards.** AFBI began a three-year [alternative forages study](https://www.afbini.gov.uk/news/new-afbi-research-launched-alternative-forages-across-northern-ireland) on 15 September. It tests clover, chicory and plantain mixes against ryegrass on eight farms from Fermanagh to Down. Legume swards could cut bagged nitrogen and its nitrous oxide.

Breeding is now the front line: the £16.6m cattle genotyping scheme opened on 1 September and will become a payment condition in 2028.

## Security: food, feed, fertiliser and energy

The carbon tax on imported fertiliser will be small at first, but the price of fertiliser itself is the bigger risk this winter.

- **UK CBAM.** HMRC's [illustrative rates](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/illustrative-cbam-rates-and-worked-example-for-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam-illustrative-cbam-rates-and-worked-example) (16 October) put fertiliser at £1.45/tCO2e, against about £9 for steel and cement. Using HMRC's [draft default values](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/check-the-default-values-for-the-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam/fertilisers-default-values), that is about £3.77 per tonne of ammonium nitrate (CN 3102 30 90, 2.600 tCO2e/t) and about £3.07 per tonne of CAN at 27% nitrogen (3102 40 10, 2.120 tCO2e/t), well below earlier estimates of £10–£50/t. Fertiliser defaults carry no mark-up. HMRC stresses these are not forecasts. The importer pays, which is usually the merchant or co-op, not the farm; check the commodity code on the invoice, because each code has its own value. Count down to the start with the [UK CBAM Countdown]({{ '/tools/uk-cbam-countdown.html' | relative_url }}).
- **Prices.** NI merchants quoted CAN at £400–405/t and urea at £555–560/t in late September ([Farmers Journal](https://www.farmersjournal.ie/more/northern-ireland/big-surge-in-fertiliser-sales-in-september-933986)). GB prices are about 35% up on a year ago, per AHDB data cited by Farmers Weekly.
- **Buying pattern.** Deliveries fell 22.0% in January to June 2026 ([DAERA](https://www.daera-ni.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-08/Northern%20Ireland%20Fertiliser%20Statistics%20for%20Q2%202026.pdf)). Then merchants reported a sales surge after almost £240m of Farm Sustainability Payments landed on 1 September ([Farmers Journal](https://www.farmersjournal.ie/northernireland/northernireland/240m-of-payments-issued-to-ni-farmers-930837)).
- **EU CBAM.** The EU's definitive regime has applied since 1 January 2026. The Commission did not suspend it for fertiliser. Its [Fertiliser Action Plan](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_1099/IP_26_1099_EN.pdf) of 19 May 2026 set a 1% mark-up on fertiliser default values, against 10% rising to 30% for other sectors. *(Corrected 11 November 2026: an earlier version said a suspension was under debate.)*
- **Animal health.** AFBI forecasts a [high liver fluke risk](https://www.afbini.gov.uk/news/high-risk-liver-fluke-infection-sheep-and-cattle-autumn-and-winter-20262027) after a summer 9% warmer and 8% wetter than average. Fluke can cut milk yield by 5–15%, which raises the carbon cost of every litre.
- **Waste and energy.** Councils recycled 53.0% of waste in April to June 2026, with landfill down to 4.8% ([DAERA](https://www.daera-ni.gov.uk/news/northern-ireland-local-authority-collected-municipal-waste-management-statistics-released-221026), 22 October). No new energy figures this month.

## Policy and deadlines coming up

The next NAP has to clear the Executive before the Assembly's last sitting day on 16 March 2027, so the rules for 2027–2030 should be known this winter.

| When | What | Who it affects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| By 1 Jan 2027 | HMRC publishes official CBAM rates for January–March 2027 | Fertiliser importers; prices to farms |
| Dec 2026 | June Agricultural Census 2026 results (provisional date) | Everyone tracking herd size |
| 1 Jan 2027 | UK CBAM starts, fertiliser included | Importers; costs pass to farms |
| Jan 2027 | Calves born in 2027 become eligible for £13 genotyping | Cattle farmers |
| 1 Feb 2027 | EU CBAM certificate sales begin | EU importers; NI supply chains |
| By 16 Mar 2027 | Executive approval of the Nutrients Action Programme 2027–2030 (consultation closed 7 Sep 2026) | All farms |
| 15 May 2027 | Soil Nutrient Health Scheme registration and training must be complete, or lose 10% of FSP | FSP claimants |
| Aug 2027 | Hillsborough emissions facility handed over | Research, then all cattle farms |
| 30 Sep 2027 | First EU CBAM declaration for 2026 imports | EU importers |
| 31 Dec 2027 | End of NI's first carbon budget; 4,500 ha peatland recovery target | All sectors, agriculture most |
| 15 May 2028 | Bovine Genetics registration and training must be complete, or lose 10% of FSP | Cattle farms claiming FSP |
| 2030 | 30% of land and inland water managed for nature | Land managers |

Still awaited: the Strategic Ammonia Reduction Programme, expected "in the autumn" ([Farmers Journal](https://www.farmersjournal.ie/more/northern-ireland/extension-to-nap-deadline-ruled-out-922945), 8 July), and a start date for the FSP carbon footprinting condition, which [DAERA](https://www.daera-ni.gov.uk/news/farm-sustainability-payment-conditionalities) says is not yet agreed.

F-gases: nothing new this month for farm refrigeration. The EU F-gas Regulation applies directly in NI ([DAERA](https://www.daera-ni.gov.uk/articles/fluorinated-greenhouse-gases)), and its new leak-check rules for refrigeration and heat pumps have applied since 23 July 2026 ([European Commission](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/areas-action/fluorinated-greenhouse-gases/documentation/f-gas-legislation_en)). Bulk tank and cold-store owners should ask their engineer which checks now apply.

## Three things a farm can do this month

1. Opt in to the Bovine Genetics genotyping scheme on the SRG portal and book the CAFRE training. It pays £13 per genotype and becomes an FSP condition by 15 May 2028.
2. Plan a fluke programme with your vet now: treat for immature and mature fluke this autumn, and check the result with a dung sample 3–4 weeks later.
3. Before buying spring fertiliser, set it against your soil results and the CBAM rates due in December. Price clover or herb swards on fields that suit them.
