Northern Ireland construction output in early 2026 was 10% above a year earlier and housing work 27% above, but NI Water says around £7bn is needed for wastewater and capacity limits decide where homes can connect. My fifth Focus on briefing covers where construction sits in the emissions inventory, why materials are the footprint, the UK carbon border levy on imported steel, aluminium and cement from 1 January (including supplies from the Republic of Ireland and the rest of the EU), the EU deforestation rules for timber from 30 December (small firms included), contaminated land, and five dates to January 2028.
