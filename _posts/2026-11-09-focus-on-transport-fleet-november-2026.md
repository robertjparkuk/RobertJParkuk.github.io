Domestic transport produced 3.9 million tonnes of CO2e in Northern Ireland in 2024, 21% of the total and 9% more than in 1990, and NI has 60 public EV chargers per 100,000 people against 175 across the UK. My fifth Focus on briefing covers charging and driver attitudes, the Electric Car Grant and company car tax, the larger licence allowance for zero emission vans, the carbon price on GB–NI ferries since 1 July, public transport passing its pre-COVID level, six dates to April 2028 and three things a fleet operator can do this month.
