The UK–EU summit in Brussels on 20 November 2026 did not conclude an agreement to link the UK and EU emissions trading systems. Talks continue. For your CBAM bill, the position is the one to plan around anyway: both charges apply as they stand.

## Where things stand

- **UK CBAM.** It starts on 1 January 2027 across the UK, including Northern Ireland. HMRC's [policy summary](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam-policy-summary/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam-policy-summary) says no jurisdiction is exempt. HMRC publishes each quarter's rates [by the start of the quarter](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-for-the-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam).
- **EU CBAM.** It has applied to imports since 1 January 2026. EU importers buy certificates from 1 February 2027 and file their first declaration by 30 September 2027 ([European Commission](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/price-cbam-certificates_en)).
- **Any future link.** Even when an agreement is reached, each side needs its own legal act before any exemption applies: UK regulations under section 156 of the [Finance Act 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/11/part/5), and an EU delegated act adding the UK to Annex III of the [CBAM Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32023R0956).

## What it means for you

- **You import steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser or hydrogen into NI, including from Ireland or the EU.** The UK CBAM applies from 1 January 2027 once your covered imports pass £50,000. EU-made goods count (Finance Act 2026, s.144(5)(a)).
- **You make goods in Northern Ireland.** They are outside the EU CBAM: the European Commission says "Goods produced and/or released for free circulation in Northern Ireland are not subject to CBAM" ([CBAM Q&A, Q 7.14](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/013fa763-5dce-4726-a204-69fec04d5ce2_en?filename=CBAM_Questions+and+Answers.pdf)).
- **You supply EU customers from Great Britain.** Your customers owe the EU CBAM on your goods and need your emissions data. Verified data usually beats the EU default values, which carry a mark-up.

## What to do in December

Set up CBAM records for 1 January, ask your suppliers for verified emissions data, and budget for both charges in 2027.
