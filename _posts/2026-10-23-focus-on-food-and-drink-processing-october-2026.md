Northern Ireland's food and drink processors turn over an estimated £7.1bn, and almost half of their sales go to Great Britain. This week's briefing covers where their emissions sit, why the farm is in every processor's footprint, and seven new rules arriving in fourteen months, from EU deforestation checks on 30 December to deposit return in October 2027.
