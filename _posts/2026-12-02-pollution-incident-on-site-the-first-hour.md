Most pollution incidents are made worse, or kept small, in the first hour. Here is a plan to print and pin by the spill kit.

## 1. Stop it, if it is safe to

Close the valve, right the drum, switch off the pump. Never put yourself or anyone else at risk.

## 2. Contain it

Spill kit, drain covers, booms. **Protect the drains first.** Anything that gets into a surface-water drain goes straight to a river or the lough.

## 3. Call

- **Fire, or any risk to people:** 999
- **Pollution of a river, lough, the sea or groundwater:** NIEA Water Pollution Hotline, **0800 80 70 60** (24 hours)
- **A spill into the foul sewer:** NI Water Waterline, **03457 440088** (24 hours)
- Your manager: ______________________
- Your environmental contact: ______________________

## 4. Record

Time, substance, rough volume, where it went, photos, and who you called and when.

## 5. Don't

- Hose it into a drain.
- Use detergent to "clear" an oil sheen. It spreads the oil and harms the water.
- Wait to see if it goes away.

## 6. After the first hour

- Report under your permit or consent if its conditions require it.
- Find out why it happened, and fix the cause, not just the spill.
- Restock the spill kit.

## Make it your own

The plan works best with your site's own details added: a drain plan with surface-water and foul drains marked in different colours, where each spill kit is kept, and the names and numbers of the people to call. If your site discharges trade effluent to sewer, or to a watercourse under a discharge consent, keep the consent numbers with this sheet.
