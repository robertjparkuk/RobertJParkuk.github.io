Northern Ireland industry emissions fell 69% between 1990 and 2024, and NI businesses sold £20.1bn to Great Britain and £14.1bn to the EU in 2024. My fourth Focus on briefing covers the UK carbon border levy on imported steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser and hydrogen from 1 January, including goods from the EU, when to register and what to record, what EU customers are asking for, where UK–EU carbon-market linking stands before the 20 November summit, and six dates to May 2028.
