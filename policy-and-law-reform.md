## Why this page exists

I advise manufacturers, councils and trade bodies on carbon, pollution, land and the rules that govern them. Doing that work, I keep finding law that was passed and never switched on, duties that overlap or contradict each other, and decisions that nobody clearly owns. Northern Ireland has had no Law Commission operating since April 2015, so these gaps tend to stay open.

This page collects my own work on closing them.

## Who pays for it

**Nobody.** This work is not funded by any client, and no client has asked for or shaped any of it. It is of interest to me as a practitioner, and I publish it so that anyone who agrees with a proposal, or wants to argue with it, can do so in the open.

If a future client's interests touch a proposal here, I will say so on that proposal and in my engagement letter. Paid public affairs work, including lobbying, is a separate service with its own terms: see [Services]({{ '/services/#public-affairs-and-communications' | relative_url }}).

## What I am working on

### Law that is on the books but not on the ground

A register of environment, infrastructure and climate law across the UK and Northern Ireland that is uncommenced, not working, bypassed, or overtaken by the courts, with a proposed single code of engagement for how such decisions are made.
[The law on the books is not the law on the ground]({% post_url 2026-11-21-the-law-on-the-books-is-not-the-law-on-the-ground %})

The clearest Northern Ireland example: the contaminated land regime in Part III of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997, which has never been commenced.
[The land law Stormont never switched on]({% post_url 2026-11-02-the-land-law-stormont-never-switched-on %})

### The public realm

A draft Public Realm Bill, written in Parliamentary Counsel style: streets, verges, parks, rivers and beaches, the places we share but don't own, and who is responsible for looking after them. It will go first to the responsible Secretary of State and the Official Opposition, then as a Private Member's Bill version, then as a concept paper for Northern Ireland's parties ahead of the May 2027 Assembly election.
[Improving the public realm: guardianship, not just maintenance]({% post_url 2026-10-17-improving-the-public-realm %})

## How I work on it

- **Drafting:** clauses and Schedules, defined terms and Explanatory Notes in the standard Westminster format, so a drafting team can work with the text.
- **Devolution:** where a proposal touches devolved competence in Northern Ireland, Scotland or Wales, or engages the Sewel convention, I say so on the proposal.
- **Sources:** primary sources only (legislation.gov.uk, Hansard, the Assembly, GOV.UK, NI departments, EUR-Lex), with a date on every claim about the current law.
- **Changes:** I revise proposals when the law changes or when someone shows me I'm wrong, and record what changed.

This is policy work, not legal advice. Where a proposal would affect a particular business or site, take advice from a solicitor.
