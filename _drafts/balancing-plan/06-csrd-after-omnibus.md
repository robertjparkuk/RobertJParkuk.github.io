Very few Northern Ireland firms are directly in scope of the EU's Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive. Most feel it **second-hand**, through questionnaires from large EU customers and lenders. The 2025–26 Omnibus simplification cut the direct scope back hard, and added a ceiling on what big customers may demand of smaller suppliers.

## Three ways CSRD reaches an NI firm

1. **Directly**: a Northern Ireland company is not itself an EU undertaking. CSRD reaches it only through an EU subsidiary in scope, or under the third-country rule for groups with over €450 million of EU turnover *[confirm on EUR-Lex]*.
2. **Through an EU parent**: your data goes into the group report.
3. **Through EU customers**: the common route. Under the Omnibus, firms averaging up to 1,000 staff can decline value-chain requests beyond the Voluntary Standard for SMEs (VSME) from FY2027.

## What that means in practice

- Answer customer requests **in VSME format** where you can. It is the ceiling for firms your size, and it overlaps heavily with what UK customers and public buyers ask.
- Keep one data set: energy, emissions (Scopes 1 and 2 at least), water, waste, workforce basics. The [Carbon Ready Reckoner](/tools/carbon-ready-reckoner.html) is a first step for the emissions part.
- Do not commission a full ESRS report unless you are actually in scope.
