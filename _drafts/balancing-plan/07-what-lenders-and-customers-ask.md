You are a 60-person firm in Co. Antrim. In one month you get a bank's ESG form, a supermarket's supplier survey and a council tender asking for a carbon reduction plan. They look different. Underneath, they ask for much the same things.

## Three formats, one data set

| Who asks | Format they lean on | What they want from you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UK listed customers and lenders | UK SRS (built on ISSB), first reports in 2028 for periods from 1 Jan 2027 | Scope 1 and 2, often Scope 3 estimates; climate risks |
| EU customers | VSME (the ceiling for firms up to 1,000 staff under the Omnibus) | Energy, emissions, water, waste, workforce basics |
| UK central government buyers (NI public buyers follow their own guidance) | Carbon reduction plan (PPN 006: UK central government contracts over £5m a year) | Scope 1, 2 and five Scope 3 categories; a net zero commitment |

## The answer template

A one-sheet download with the 25 data points that answer most requests, each tagged with which format uses it, and a column for your figure and its source. Fill it once a year; copy from it all year.
