**PRINT AND PIN BY THE SPILL KIT**

**1. Stop it, if safe.** Close the valve, right the drum, switch off the pump.

**2. Contain it.** Spill kit, drain covers, booms. Protect drains first: anything in a surface-water drain goes straight to a river.

**3. Call.**
- Fire or risk to people: **999**
- Water pollution: NIEA pollution hotline **0800 80 70 60** *[verify]*
- Spill to foul sewer: NI Water *[number to add]*
- Your manager: ______  Your environmental contact: ______

**4. Record.** Time, substance, rough volume, where it went, photos, who you called.

**5. Don't.** Hose it into a drain. Use detergent to "clear" an oil sheen. Wait to see if it goes away.

**6. After.** Report under your permit or consent if required; review why it happened; restock the kit.
