I draft legislation in the style of the Office of Parliamentary Counsel: clauses, Schedules, defined terms and Explanatory Notes. Three pieces of work are in progress. They are private proposals, not Government Bills.

## Public Realm (Framework and Entitlement) Bill
**The problem:** streets, verges, parks, rivers and beaches are everyone's and no one's. Duties to look after them are scattered across dozens of old Acts.
**The idea:** a short framework Bill with a continuous-improvement duty, minimum public realm entitlements, and land value capture to pay for it. The older amenity laws are left to a separate consolidation project (the Law Commission covers England and Wales; Scotland and Northern Ireland would need their own routes ; the Northern Ireland Law Commission has not operated since April 2015).
**Where it stands:** reworked after a scrutiny exercise in October 2026 into a shorter framework Bill. For the UK Government and the Official Opposition first; then a Private Member's Bill version; then a concept paper for Northern Ireland's parties before the May 2027 Assembly election. Devolution is respected throughout: provisions that touch devolved matters are flagged for legislative consent.

## National Infrastructure Bill
**The problem:** energy, grid, rail, roads, water, housing and new towns are planned in silos, and big projects stall between departments and inquiries.
**The idea:** one machinery-of-government framework for major national projects: a statutory infrastructure body, a public value test, regular reports laid before Parliament for audit scrutiny, and a parliamentary route for projects of strategic national significance, while respecting the devolved settlements.
**Where it stands:** working draft, started November 2026; to be positioned against the Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025.

## Repeal, Replace, Renew: a review of environment, climate, infrastructure and public realm law
**The problem:** law in force but never commenced or quietly bypassed, such as Part III of the Waste and Contaminated Land (NI) Order 1997.
**The idea:** a register of dormant and bypassed law, a procedural code of engagement, and a duty to commence a law within five years rather than an automatic sunset.
**Where it stands:** register and draft Bill in progress; first findings in *The law on the books is not the law on the ground*.
