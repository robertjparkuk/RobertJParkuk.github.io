Two different questions, two different numbers.

| | Organisation inventory | Product footprint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Answers | How much does the whole business emit in a year? | How much is in one unit of what we sell? |
| Standard | GHG Protocol Corporate; ISO 14064-1 | GHG Protocol Product; ISO 14067; EN 15804 (EPDs) |
| Unit | tonnes CO2e per year, by Scope 1, 2, 3 | kg CO2e per tonne, per item, per m² |
| Who asks | lenders, UK SRS reporters, public buyers, VSME | customers comparing products, specifiers, CBAM (embedded emissions) |
| Start with | a year of bills; the [Reckoner](/tools/carbon-ready-reckoner.html) | your inventory, then allocate to products |

**CBAM** sits on the product side: it asks for embedded emissions per tonne of goods, by installation. An organisation inventory does not answer it, but its data helps.

**Rule of thumb:** do the organisation inventory first. It is cheaper, it is what most requests want, and it is the data a product footprint is built from.
