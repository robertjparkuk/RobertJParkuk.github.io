## Why this sector now
It sits where four regimes meet: packaging EPR (UK, in force 1 Jan 2025 including NI), the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (applies in NI since 12 Aug 2026), EU chemicals rules including PFAS restrictions (apply to goods in NI under the Windsor Framework), and UK REACH in GB.

## Sections
1. Sector in numbers *[NISRA]*
2. Two packaging rulebooks for one product: what changes for an NI converter selling into GB and Ireland
3. PFAS in process aids, coatings and fluoropolymers: the firefighting-foam duties already applying, the universal restriction to come
4. Energy and emissions: process heat, compressed air, extrusion
5. Customers' questions: recycled content, recyclability claims, Scope 3
6. Three actions this quarter
