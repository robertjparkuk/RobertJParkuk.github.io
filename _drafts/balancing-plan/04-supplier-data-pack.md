## Covering letter (plain text, for email)

Subject: Carbon border data for goods you supply to us

Dear [supplier],

From 1 January 2027 the UK charges a carbon border adjustment (CBAM) on imports of iron and steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers and hydrogen. We buy [goods] from you, and we may be the importer for these goods.

To report accurately, and to avoid paying on default values that may overstate your emissions, please complete the attached sheet for each installation that makes goods you sell us. If you already complete the EU CBAM communication template for EU customers, please send that instead.

If you cannot provide figures yet, please tell us when you expect to. We are not asking for confidential process information beyond what the template requires.

Kind regards, [name]

## The sheet (see companion CSV)

One row per product and installation: supplier, installation name and address, country, CN code, product description, production route, reporting period, direct embedded emissions (tCO2e per tonne), indirect embedded emissions, source of the figures (measured, calculated, default), verified (yes/no), verifier and accreditation body, carbon price paid in country of origin, contact.
