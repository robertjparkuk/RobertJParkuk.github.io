PFAS are thousands of very persistent fluorinated chemicals. In Northern Ireland, EU chemicals rules apply to goods under the Windsor Framework; GB follows UK REACH. On **23 October 2026** the first duties under the EU's ban on PFAS in firefighting foam started to apply in Northern Ireland. The UK Government, replying to the Environmental Audit Committee on 6 July 2026, said it would wait for the EU's universal restriction before deciding on a group-wide approach.

## Where to look first
| Where | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Fire systems and stores | Do any foams contain PFAS? Since when, and what replaces them? |
| Coatings, sprays, release agents | Fluoropolymer or fluorotelomer content on the safety data sheet? |
| Lubricants and greases | PTFE-based? |
| Seals, gaskets, hoses, membranes | Fluoropolymer parts in process equipment |
| Textiles and PPE | Water- or stain-repellent finishes |
| Packaging | Grease-resistant papers |
| Effluent | Is anything above reaching your discharge? |

## Then
1. Ask suppliers in writing about PFAS content.
2. Separate "uses we can stop" from "uses with no alternative yet".
3. Check which EU and UK restrictions reach each product you sell, and when.
