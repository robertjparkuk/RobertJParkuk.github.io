*Clearly fictional: "Larne Steelworks Ltd", 45 staff, makes structural steel frames and agricultural gates.*

## Step 1: Boundary
Two sites, one owned, one leased. Operational control. Reporting year: calendar 2026.

## Step 2: Data request
Twelve months of gas (LPG for the paint oven), electricity, kerosene for office heating, red diesel for the yard telehandler, fleet fuel cards, refrigerant service records, waste transfer notes, water bills, purchase ledger by supplier.

## Step 3: First estimate in the Reckoner
Scopes 1 and 2 and the partial Scope 3 from the Reckoner. *[Run and insert figures.]*

## Step 4: The big one: purchased steel
Purchased goods (Scope 3 category 1) will dwarf everything else: on the order of 1.5 to 2.5 tonnes of CO2e per tonne of blast-furnace steel, far less for electric-arc steel *[verify ranges against worldsteel or supplier EPDs]*. For 2,000 tonnes a year, that is thousands of tonnes, against perhaps 200 to 400 for Scopes 1 and 2.

## Step 5: What the board does with it
- Supplier choice becomes a carbon lever: ask mills for EPDs.
- Paint-line LPG is the biggest on-site source: costed options for heat recovery or electrification.
- One data set answers the bank, the EU customer (VSME) and the council tender.

## Step 6: Verification
If a customer or lender needs it, an independent UKAS-accredited verifier gives their own opinion. I prepare the data; I do not verify it.
