Northern Ireland is not on the Great Britain grid. It shares the **Single Electricity Market** with Ireland, so wholesale prices, network charges and the carbon figure for your electricity all work differently from GB.

## What you are paying for *[verify shares]*
- **Energy**: the wholesale price in the all-island market
- **Networks**: NIE Networks distribution and SONI transmission charges
- **Levies and policy costs**: including the Public Service Obligation levy
- **Capacity**: payments that keep enough generation available
- **Supplier margin**

## Five questions to ask before you renew
1. When does the contract end, and what happens if you miss the window?
2. Fixed, flexible or pass-through: which risks are you carrying?
3. Are your network charges on the right tariff for your load?
4. Is your power factor costing you?
5. What is the supplier's fuel mix? It sets your market-based Scope 2 figure.

## The carbon figure
DESNZ does not publish an NI location-based factor. The Reckoner uses the UK factor as a disclosed proxy and your supplier's fuel mix from the Utility Regulator for the market-based figure.
