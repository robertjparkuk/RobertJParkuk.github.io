The prototype converts a year of fuels and electricity to kWh, divides by tonnes produced, and compares up to three years, with the change in percent. It deliberately shows **no sector benchmark**: published benchmarks for small NI manufacturers are thin and mixing products makes them misleading. Panel to advise whether to add one (e.g. from DESNZ ECUK or trade-body data).

Runs in the browser; nothing stored on a server.
