**Who:** large undertakings, meaning 250 or more staff, or turnover above £44 million with a balance sheet above £38 million, and the UK group they belong to. Small firms in a large group are caught through the group.

**When:** qualification is judged on **31 December 2026**; notification is due by **5 December 2027**.

**What has changed since Phase 3:** progress updates now need sign-off by a board-level director, and DESNZ has promised a consultation on aligning SECR and ESOS reporting *[verify status]*.

**Northern Ireland specifics:**
- Your electricity sits on the all-island market, so savings and carbon are valued differently from GB (see item 28).
- An ESOS audit, a carbon inventory and a contracts review use the same data: do them once.

**Making it pay:** the audit is only worth its cost if the board acts on it. Rank opportunities by payback and carbon; put the top three in the next budget; report progress in the annual update.

**How I deliver it:** I lead the work with ESOS assessors; a Lead Assessor signs off. I am not a Lead Assessor myself.
