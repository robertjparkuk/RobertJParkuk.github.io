Every litre that leaves your site goes one of two ways, and each has its own permission.

| Where it goes | Permission | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| To the public sewer | Trade effluent consent | NI Water *[verify legal basis]* |
| To a river, lough, sea or soakaway | Discharge consent | NIEA *[verify]* |

## Why it matters more from 2027
- **Cost:** NI Water's price limit for 2027/28 is 3.4% above inflation (Utility Regulator, 21 Aug 2026), and trade effluent charges sit within it. Charges rise with volume and strength.
- **Standards:** DAERA plans new discharge standards across NI (announced 3 March 2026).
- **Connection risk:** where sewers and treatment works are at capacity, new or bigger loads may be refused.

## Five things to check this year
1. Do you hold a consent for every discharge, and do its conditions still match your process?
2. Is your measured flow and strength close to what the consent allows?
3. Which process step makes the strongest effluent (wash-down, CIP, rinsing)?
4. Could yard rainwater be kept out of the foul sewer?
5. What would a spill do, and who would you call? (see item 23)
