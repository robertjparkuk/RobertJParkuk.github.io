Who decides?

Northern Ireland sits under UK law and, for goods, parts of EU law. This map shows who decides what on the environment, carbon and trade.

UK Government and Parliament

DESNZUK ETS, UK CBAM policy (with HM Treasury and HMRC), ESOS and SECR.
HM Treasury and HMRCUK CBAM rates and collection; Climate Change Levy; tax.
DefraUK-wide packaging EPR (with DAERA for NI); UK REACH (GB).
Cabinet Office and NIOWindsor Framework implementation; UK–EU Joint Committee.

NI Executive and Assembly

DAERAEnvironment, climate (carbon budgets, Climate Action Plan), waste, water quality, agriculture.
NIEA (part of DAERA)Pollution control, permits, discharge consents, waste regulation, enforcement.
DfEEnergy strategy, biomethane, the electricity market policy.
DfIPlanning policy, roads, water and sewerage policy.
DfCHistoric environment.

Regulators and bodies

Utility RegulatorElectricity, gas and water price controls in NI; NI Water's price limit.
SEM CommitteeAll-island wholesale electricity market (with Ireland's CRU).
NI WaterTrade effluent consents; drinking water supply.

PlanningLocal Development Plans and most planning decisions.

EU and the Windsor Framework

EU law that applies in NIFor goods: rules on products, chemicals (REACH), packaging (PPWR), and more.
